The Mission

Millions of Ukrainians have been displaced, needing safety and basic necessities.

We help Ukrainian hospitals, schools, bomb shelters, rehabilitation centres and people rebuilding their lives. Whilst ensuring tonnes of quality UK waste is diverted from landfill.

Our Work

Collecting & Delivering Needed Aid

We are a group of British and Ukrainian volunteers. Partnering with local communities, schools, medical centres, gyms, and hotels across the UK to collect donations.

Items are systematically sorted at our South England hub, securely packed, and dispatched straight to Ukraine.

We partner with small, trusted Ukrainian charities in Kyiv, Odessa, and Sumy, which make the final mile delivery to communities closer to the frontline.
